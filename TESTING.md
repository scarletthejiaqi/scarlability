# Scarlability — What to Test

Everything to try on your iPad, grouped by part. Do it all at the end.
Tick a box when it works. If it doesn't, write what happened next to it (a screenshot helps a lot).

---

## 0. One-time setup (already done once)
1. Plug the iPad into the Mac and unlock it.
2. In Xcode, choose your iPad at the top center and press **⌘R**.
3. If the iPad says "Untrusted Developer": **Settings → General → VPN & Device Management →** your Apple ID **→ Trust**, then ⌘R again.
4. Every 7 days the app stops opening. Plug in the iPad and press ⌘R again; your notes are kept.

## 1. First launch and saving (M1)
- [ ] The first screen asks where to keep notes. Pick **iCloud Drive**.
- [ ] **Files app → iCloud Drive → Scarlability** contains **"Test Note"** as **one item**, not a folder full of files.
- [ ] Write something, swipe the app closed, and reopen it. The writing is still there.

## 2. Pen (M1)
- [ ] **Smooth:** write slowly, then fast. It's one smooth curve, not small straight pieces joined together.
- [ ] **No flash:** when you lift the Pencil, the line doesn't change (no bold "highlight" that then gets thinner).
- [ ] **Pressure:** light writing is thinner, pressing hard is thicker.
- [ ] **Lag:** when you write fast, the ink stays close to the Pencil tip.
- [ ] **Palm:** rest your hand on the screen while writing. The page doesn't move and no marks appear.
- [ ] **Zoomed in:** zoom in and write. The ink is sharp.

## 3. Toolbar (M1, redesigned in M4.5)
- [ ] **Colors:** the second row has black, blue, red, white, pink, orange, gray. Tap the **selected** color again and the color picker opens. Try the eyedropper (吸管) in it.
- [ ] **Extra color:** tap the gray **+**, pick a color, and it becomes the 8th color.
- [ ] **Sizes:** the 3 size dots are after the colors. Tap one. Tap the **selected** dot again and a thickness slider appears.
- [ ] **⋯ → Settings → Pressure sensitivity:** move the slider, then write. You can feel the difference.
- [ ] **Hand button** (✋ after the line in the toolbar) = draw with finger: when it's blue, one finger writes and two fingers scroll. Tap it again to turn it off.
- [ ] **Undo/redo buttons** (arrows at top right) work.

## 4. Pages and zoom (M1)
- [ ] There's always a blank page at the end. Writing on it adds another.
- [ ] **Pages connect:** write across the line between two pages. The writing continues onto the next page.
- [ ] Undoing a stroke that crossed two pages removes all of it at once.
- [ ] **Zoom lock (锁定):** pinch slowly. The page **sticks** when it fills the screen edge to edge, with **no gray edge (边) on the sides**. In landscape it also sticks when the whole page fits. Keep pinching and it moves on.
- [ ] **Two-finger tap** undoes. **Three-finger tap** redoes.
- [ ] Rotate to landscape. Nothing looks broken.

## 5. Highlighter (M2)
- [ ] Tap **Highlighter** and highlight across some writing. The yellow stays **behind** the writing.
- [ ] Highlight the same spot twice, or cross two highlights. The color stays **even** (no darker spots once you lift).
- [ ] It's a flat chisel tip: highlighting sideways gives a tall band, and going up and down is thinner.
- [ ] Draw a highlight and **hold** the Pencil still at the end. It snaps to a **straight line**.
- [ ] Colors: yellow plus the rainbow **+**. Tap the selected color again for the picker and eyedropper (取色笔).
- [ ] The 3 sizes work, and tapping the selected size again shows its slider.

## 6. Eraser (M2)
- [ ] **Whole Stroke** (the default): touching a line removes all of it.
- [ ] **Partial**: rubs out just the part you touch, so a line can be cut in two.
- [ ] **Highlighter only**: erases highlights and leaves the writing.
- [ ] A gray circle shows where and how big the eraser is. The 3 sizes work.
- [ ] One eraser swipe undoes in **one** step.
- [ ] You stay on the eraser after lifting the Pencil.

## 7. Select (M2)
- [ ] **Freeform**: draw a loop around some writing. It gets a dashed box and a bar of buttons pops up **above** it (not covering it), centered.
- [ ] **Box**: drag a rectangle to select.
- [ ] **Move**: drag inside the box, with the Pencil or a finger.
- [ ] **Resize**: drag the bottom-right circle. **Rotate**: drag the circle above the box.
- [ ] Bar buttons in this order: **Delete, Copy, Paste** (only after copying something), **Cut, Duplicate, Color, Thickness, Copy as Image**. Nothing is hidden behind a ▸ arrow. If the bar is wider than the screen, swipe it left and right.
- [ ] **Copy as Image**, then paste into Messages or Notes. A picture of the selection appears.
- [ ] Copy, then tap an empty spot with the Select tool. A **Paste** button appears there.
- [ ] Scroll or zoom while something is selected. The bar follows the selection.
- [ ] Tap outside the selection to deselect it. Undo puts moved or deleted ink back.
- [ ] Select writing that crosses two pages, then move it. It stays together.

## 8. Shapes (M2)
- [ ] With the pen, draw a rough line, circle, oval, rectangle, triangle or other polygon and **hold still about half a second**. It snaps to a clean shape.
- [ ] Normal handwriting never snaps by accident.

## 9. Paper (M2)
- [ ] Tap the **paper icon** (top right, next to ⋯). Switch between **Blank** and **Lined**, and try the **Narrow / Medium / Wide** line spacing.
- [ ] Try the colors **White, Cream, Gray, Dark**. On Dark paper, black ink shows as **white**.
- [ ] **Whole Note** changes every page, and new pages get it too. **This Page** changes only the page in the middle of the screen.

## 10. Apple Pencil double-tap (M2)
- [ ] Double-tapping the Pencil does what **iPad Settings → Apple Pencil → Double-tap** is set to (switch to the eraser and back, switch to the previous tool, or show the color picker).

## 11. Library (M3, redesigned in M4.5 to look like Notability)
- [ ] The app opens to the **library**: on the left a gear (settings), a **Search** box, **Notes** with how many you have, **Folders**, and **Recently Deleted** at the bottom. On the right a big **All Notes** title with tabs and your notes as cards.
- [ ] Tabs: **All Notes**, **Recents** (opened or changed in the last 30 days, newest first), **Favorites**, **Unfiled** (notes in no folder).
- [ ] Each card shows the top of the first page, the title, the date, and a colored tag with its folder (in the tabs).
- [ ] **Folders:** each has a colored dot. A **folder group** (like "Completed course") has a ▾ arrow. Tap it to fold its folders away and back.
- [ ] **+ next to Folders → New Folder**: a name, one of 9 colors, and optionally a group to put it in. **New Folder Group** makes a group.
- [ ] Long-press a folder: **Rename, Color, Move to Group, Delete**. Deleting sends its notes to Recently Deleted.
- [ ] Long-press a folder group: **New Folder Inside, Rename, Color, Delete**.
- [ ] Tap the blue **+ New → Note** (top right) for a new note named like "Note Oct 9, 2026 4:30 PM". It goes in the folder you're viewing (or Unfiled). **+ New → Folder** makes a folder.
- [ ] Tap a note to open it. The **←** button goes back, and the thumbnail shows your new writing.
- [ ] In a note, **⋯ → Rename Note**.
- [ ] Long-press a note: **Rename, Add to Favorites (★), Move to…, Duplicate, Delete**. A favorite gets a yellow ★ on its card and shows in the Favorites tab. **Remove from Favorites** takes it out.
- [ ] **Drag** a note's card onto a folder in the sidebar to move it.
- [ ] **⋯ (top right) → Select Notes**: tap several notes, then **Favorite**, **Move** or **Delete** them together.
- [ ] **⋯ → Sort by**: Date Modified (default), Date Created, Title.
- [ ] **Search** (left) finds notes by title in every folder. Clear it with the ⓧ.
- [ ] **Recently Deleted**: each note shows how many of its 30 days are left. Tap one for **Restore** or **Delete Forever**. **⋯ → Empty Recently Deleted** clears it all.
- [ ] Restoring puts a note back in its subject (or in Unfiled if that subject is gone).
- [ ] **Files app → iCloud Drive → Scarlability** has a folder per divider, a folder per subject inside it, each note as one item, and a "Recently Deleted" folder.

## 12. Text (M4)
- [ ] Tap **Text** in the toolbar, then tap the page. A text box starts there and the keyboard opens.
- [ ] Type. The box grows wider as you type, then wraps at the page edge.
- [ ] Drag the **blue handle** on the box's right edge to set its width.
- [ ] While typing, the box stays above the keyboard.
- [ ] **Font** menu (System, Serif, Rounded, Monospaced, Handwritten, Marker) and **size** menu (17 pt by default).
- [ ] **Colors:** 3 presets plus the rainbow **+**. Tap the selected color again for the picker and eyedropper.
- [ ] **B / I / U**: select some words and tap, or tap first and then type.
- [ ] **Bullets** and **numbers** (list buttons). Return continues the list; Return on an empty item ends it.
- [ ] Tap outside the box to finish. The text sits **under** your handwriting, so you can write and highlight over it.
- [ ] Tap an existing box with the Text tool to edit it again. Deleting all its text removes the box.
- [ ] With **Select**: tap a text box to select it, then move, resize (the text scales), rotate, recolor, **Edit Text**, Delete.
- [ ] Copy text in another app, then **Image ▸ Paste** (or tap an empty spot with Select ▸ Paste). It becomes a text box.

## 13. Images (M4)
- [ ] **Image ▸ Photos**: pick one or more photos. They appear in the middle of the screen, about half a page wide, already selected.
- [ ] **Image ▸ Take Photo** (allow camera access the first time) and **Image ▸ Files** work.
- [ ] Copy a picture in another app, then **Image ▸ Paste**.
- [ ] Move, resize and rotate a picture with the Select tool.
- [ ] **Crop**: select a picture, choose **Crop**, drag its edges or corners in, then **Done** (or **Cancel**).
- [ ] Pictures sit **under** handwriting: write and highlight on top of them.
- [ ] **Image ▸ Scan Document**: scan 1–2 pages. They become **new pages** after the page on screen, and you can write on them.
- [ ] Undo removes an inserted picture, a crop or a text change.
- [ ] Close and reopen the note: text and pictures are still there, and the library thumbnail shows them.

## 13b. Note screen and smoother writing (M4.5)
- [ ] **Look:** the page fills the screen. Floating on top: **←** (left), the tool palette (middle: pen, highlighter, eraser, Aa text, lasso, ⊕ insert, ✋ finger drawing, with the current tool's colors and sizes below), and **undo, redo, ⋯, pages** (right). No status bar.
- [ ] **⋯** has **Rename Note, Paper, Settings**.
- [ ] **Page number** at the bottom right ("2 / 5"). The ⌃ and ⌄ arrows jump to the page above and below.
- [ ] **Page panel:** tap the pages button (top right, two rectangles). Thumbnails of every page appear on the right and the page shrinks to fit next to them. The current page has a blue border. Tap a thumbnail to jump there. Write something; its thumbnail updates a moment later.
- [ ] In the panel, the dashed **+** adds a blank page at the end and jumps to it.
- [ ] Long-press a thumbnail → **Delete Page** → confirm. The page is gone, also after closing and reopening the note.
- [ ] **No lag (不拖):** write fast, long cursive lines. The line stays right under the Pencil tip; it doesn't trail behind, rubber-band (拉扯) or shrink back (往回缩) when you lift.
- [ ] Long strokes (a whole line of handwriting without lifting) stay just as smooth at the end as at the start.
- [ ] Rotate to landscape: the palette is centered, everything fits.

## 14. Questions to answer while testing
1. **Pressure feel:** too subtle, too strong, or right compared to Notability?
2. **Smoothing:** too much (letters look rounded), too little (still bumpy), or right?
3. **Pen sizes:** thin 1.4 / medium 2.2 / thick 3.4. Do they match what you use?
4. **Colors:** are black, blue, red the right defaults?
5. **Lined paper:** 8 mm spacing, light blue lines, no red margin line. Should it look different?
6. **Zoom range:** 50%–500%. Enough?
7. **Zoom lock:** too sticky, not sticky enough, or right? (You have to pinch about 7% past it.)
8. **Undo taps:** did any get missed, or fire by accident while writing?
9. **Color picker:** is Apple's standard picker OK, or do you want a round color wheel?
10. **Settings:** is the **⋯** button a good place for pressure, finger drawing and the folder?

**From M2** (I picked a default for each):
11. **Highlighter opacity** is 38%. On Dark paper highlights look dim. More or less see-through?
12. **Highlighter while drawing:** where a new highlight crosses an old one, it looks darker until you lift, then evens out. Bothersome?
13. **Highlighter tip:** going up and down is 30% as thick as going sideways. Want a round tip instead, or a different ratio?
14. **Sizes:** highlighter 10 / 16 / 24 and eraser 4 / 9 / 18. Right?
15. **Selecting:** a line counts as selected when at least half of it is inside your loop. Too strict or too loose?
16. **Shape snap:** hold still about 0.5 s within about 3 points. Too easy or too hard to trigger?
17. **Duplicate** puts the copy a little down and to the right. OK?

**From M3** (I picked a default for each):
18. **New note names:** file names can't contain ":", so in the Files app "4:30 PM" shows as "4-30 PM". The title inside the app keeps the ":". OK?
19. **Sidebar order:** subjects without a divider come first, then dividers, each in the order you made them. Do you want drag-to-reorder?
20. **Thumbnails** show the first page. OK, or show the page you last wrote on?
21. **Duplicates** are named "… copy". OK?
22. **Search** only looks at titles (searching handwriting isn't in v1). Fine for now?
23. **Changes from your other device** show up when you come back to the library or reopen the app. Instant updates come with sync in M7. OK until then?

**From M4** (I picked a default for each):
24. **Text box outline:** a blue outline shows only while typing; finished text has no border. OK?
25. **Scanned pages** can't be undone, but since M4.5 you can delete them from the page panel. OK?
26. **Pictures** are saved at up to 2400 pixels (JPEG, or PNG if the original was PNG) to save iCloud space. Sharp enough?
27. **Zooming while typing** finishes the text box first, to keep the text sharp. OK?
28. **Text and lines:** 17 pt text doesn't line up exactly with the lined paper. Should text snap to the lines?
29. **Rotated text boxes** can be edited, but their width handle is hidden. Fine?

**From M4.5** (I picked a default for each):
30. **First page under the toolbar:** the first page starts just below the floating toolbar. When you scroll, pages slide under it. Or should page 1 also start right at the top, under the toolbar, like Notability?
31. **Pen and highlighter colors:** 7 pen colors (black, blue, red, white, pink, orange, gray) and 5 highlighter colors (yellow, green, blue, pink, orange). Right ones?
32. **Deleting a page** asks first and can't be undone. Want undo for it instead of the question?
33. **Recents** = notes you opened or changed in the last 30 days. Right window?
34. **Folder groups** only hold folders, not notes (you chose this). Still right after using it?
